EPCM TERMINAL CASE STUDY

Crude Terminal Tankage & Metering Expansion

JACHINS Development Limited acted as lead EPCM consultant for the major capacity upgrade of a strategic coastal oil export terminal handling crude storage and tanker offloading.

The scope encompassed detailed engineering design for two additional 250,000-barrel floating-roof storage tanks, integration of automated LACT (Lease Automatic Custody Transfer) fiscal metering skids, hydraulic upgrade of marine loading arms, and complete overhaul of the fire-water deluge system. The expanded terminal doubled crude handling throughput while achieving 98% budget adherence.
