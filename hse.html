Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

OUR CORE COMMITMENT

Target Zero: Safety Without Compromise

At JACHINS Development Limited, Health, Safety, and Environmental management is embedded into every operational workflow, from engineering design to field execution.

We believe that all workplace injuries, occupational illnesses, and environmental incidents are preventable. Our comprehensive Health, Safety & Environment Management System (HSE-MS) ensures strict compliance with national regulations (NUPRC, NMDPRA, NCDMB) and international standards (ISO 14001 & ISO 45001).

HSE Policy Statement

  • Zero Harm Culture: Prioritizing the health, safety, and physical well-being of employees, contractors, and local communities.
  • Environmental Stewardship: Minimizing ecological footprint through emission reduction, waste recycling, and spill prevention controls.
  • Stop Work Authority: Empowering every team member on-site to halt any activity deemed unsafe without fear of reprisal.

Key Pillars of Safety Excellence

Occupational Health

Continuous health screening, ergonomics assessment, and medical emergency readiness across off-shore and on-shore sites.

Environmental Protection

Rigorous environmental impact assessments (EIA), flare minimization strategies, and bio-friendly waste disposal protocols.

Asset Safety & HAZOP

Mandatory Hazard & Operability (HAZOP) studies, pressure testing, and integrity audits before commissioning facilities.

Emergency Response

24/7 incident response desk, emergency evacuation drills, and joint emergency drills with municipal rescue bodies.
