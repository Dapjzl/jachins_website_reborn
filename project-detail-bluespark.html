ASSET OPTIMIZATION CASE STUDY

Wireline Electro-Hydraulic Well Stimulation

JACHINS Development Limited successfully spearheaded the deployment of BlueSpark pulsed-power electro-hydraulic technology across multi-zone oilfield reservoirs in Nigeria.

Traditional chemical and acidizing stimulation methods frequently carry high logistics overheads and potential matrix damage risks. Our non-damaging acoustic technology generates ultra-high-pressure shockwaves inside the wellbore, clearing near-wellbore skin damage, removing scale deposits, and re-establishing oil flow pathways without damaging formation permeability.
